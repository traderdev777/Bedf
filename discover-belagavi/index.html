Tri-State Strategic Gateway

Situated at the tri-junction of Karnataka, Maharashtra, and Goa, placing companies within rapid reach of Pune, Mumbai, Hubballi, and Goa seaports.

Pleasant Year-Round Climate

Resting at 762m altitude on the Sahyadri foothills, Belagavi enjoys temperate, sub-tropical weather that promotes high workforce comfort and low HVAC cooling costs.

Perennial Water Catchments

Nourished by the Krishna, Ghataprabha, and Malaprabha rivers along with the 37.7 TMC Renuka Sagar reservoir, securing year-round industrial and municipal water.

Aequs Precision Engineering SEZ

India's first notified precision engineering Special Economic Zone

Spread across 250+ acres at Hattargi, Aequs is an integrated aerospace manufacturing ecosystem supplying critical machined aerostructure components to Airbus, Boeing, Safran, Collins Aerospace, and Bombardier.

Key Highlights
  • Machining, surface treatment & assembly
  • 250+ acre campus
  • Global aerospace supply chain

Governance

Suvarna Vidhana Soudha

The monumental legislative building of Karnataka in Belagavi, inaugurated in 2012, symbolizing administrative decentralization and North Karnataka leadership.

Urban Landscapes

Rani Chennamma Circle

The vibrant heart of Belagavi city, featuring the iconic equestrian statue of Rani Chennamma of Kittur, pioneer of Indian freedom.

Photo: Djsonu007Creative
Natural Wonders

Gokak Falls (Ghataprabha River)

A breathtaking 52-meter waterfall formed by the Ghataprabha River leaping over sandstone cliffs, site of one of India’s earliest hydroelectric stations (1887).

Photo: ShishirmkCreative
Heritage

Kittur Fort & Palace Ruins

Historic seat of the Kittur kingdom where Rani Chennamma waged sovereign resistance against British colonial expansion in 1824.

Photo: Pixabay / Wikimedia Commons ContributorCreative
Infrastructure

Belagavi Airport (IXG) Sambra

North Karnataka’s premier operational commercial airport with a 2,300m runway, handling regional jet airliners connecting Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, and Delhi.

Photo: Wikimedia Commons ContributorCreative
Heritage

Belagavi Fort & Moat

Constructed in 1204 CE by Jaya Raya of the Ratta dynasty, featuring massive stone bastions, a deep protective moat, and interfaith historic shrines.

Photo: Col. James Welsh (Historical Collection)Public
Heritage

Kamala Basti (Kamal Basadi)

Exquisite 10th-century Chalukyan-style Jain temple inside Belagavi Fort, famous for its magnificent 72-petal inverted stone lotus ceiling dome.

Photo: Mallikarjun H. SarviCreative
Academic

Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU)

Headquarters of Karnataka’s apex engineering university, governing 200+ engineering institutions and pioneering applied research across the state.

Raja Lakhamagouda Dam (Hidkal Dam)

Major reservoir across the Ghataprabha River in Hukkeri taluk, irrigating hundreds of thousands of acres of agricultural fertile plains.

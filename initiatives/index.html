Institutional Clarification & Disclaimer

Belagavi Economic Development Foundation (BEDF) is an independent, non-governmental, citizen-led regional platform. BEDF does not directly allot industrial land, grant statutory government licences, or guarantee state subsidies or commercial profits. All figures and geographical locations displayed on this website are sourced from published authoritative records (Census 2011, KIADB, AAI, VTU, Karnataka Gazette).

Explore our 7 structured programmes turning regional ambition into measurable economic outcomes. Every initiative features verified progress updates and citizen participation pathways.

Technology• Programme ID: ai-network
Status: Under Development

Belagavi AI Innovation Network

Uniting computer science faculties, industry automation engineers, and software founders to build localized applied AI solutions.

Program Deliverables & Objectives

  • Establish an applied AI sandbox for manufacturing quality inspection.
  • Conduct quarterly hands-on hackathons with VTU and engineering colleges.
  • Provide technical workshops on LLMs, Edge AI, and industrial IoT.

Audited Progress Log

Oct 2026Architecture and curriculum whitepaper completed for college AI clubs.
Nov 2026Technical advisory panel formed with regional software architects.
Participation Scope: Open to software engineers, AI researchers, engineering students, and forward-looking MSME founders.

Aerospace• Programme ID: aerospace-initiative
Status: Proposed Opportunity

Aerospace Development Initiative

Expanding Belagavi's aerospace footprint beyond Hattargi into avionics, tooling, and commercial aircraft components.

Program Deliverables & Objectives

  • Publish an investment feasibility dossier for narrow-body aircraft line maintenance at Sambra.
  • Assist local precision foundries in achieving aerospace heat treatment accreditation.
  • Facilitate partnerships with national aviation research establishments.

Audited Progress Log

Sep 2026Formulated baseline proposal for aerospace component testing laboratory.
Participation Scope: Aerospace component suppliers, precision machining firms, avionics engineers, and aviation authorities.

Research• Programme ID: economic-research
Status: Active

Economic Research Programme

Publishing empirical, data-driven economic dossiers on North Karnataka's manufacturing clusters, export indices, and infrastructure needs.

Program Deliverables & Objectives

  • Present policy recommendation papers to state and central planning authorities.
  • Maintain a verified open-access repository of regional economic datasets.

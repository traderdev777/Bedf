## 🛡️ Institutional Integrity Guarantee

- **Authenticity Policy**: No generic stock images from unrelated Indian or international cities are presented as Belagavi.
- **License Compliance**: All Wikimedia Commons photographs are attributed with explicit links to original authors and open-source licenses.
- **Fair Use & Attribution**: Stakeholders wishing to review or contribute official archival photography are encouraged to contact `contact@bedf.org`.
